A few useful details.

Do my files leave my computer?

Normal operation stays local. DumpToTxt has no account service, application telemetry or network tokenization. You decide where to share the result. Clipboard destinations and the apps used to open output have their own behavior and privacy policies.

Will it catch every secret?

No. Full and Compact support sensitive-data scanning and redaction, but detection is not complete. Paths and filenames are not scanned. Inspect the finished artifact before sharing it. Lite does not include sensitive-data protection.

What should I expect during installation?

Setup requests administrator approval. The current installers are unsigned, so Windows may show an unknown publisher or SmartScreen prompt. Full includes its runtime; Compact needs the .NET 8 Desktop Runtime for x64. Re-running setup lets you update, reinstall or uninstall.

Where can I get help or report a problem?

Open an issue on GitHub with the steps to reproduce the problem. Check any example output or logs for private information before posting them.
