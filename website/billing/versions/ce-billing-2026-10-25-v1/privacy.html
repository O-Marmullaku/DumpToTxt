Data and purposes

We process the billing and company contact details you provide, the selected product and offer, order/customer/subscription/payment references, amounts, currencies, tax and location evidence, payment and cancellation status, refunds or disputes, and relevant correspondence. A particular offer may require a business tax ID or company employee band. We receive transaction information from the checkout/payment provider and relevant product systems as well as information you send directly.

We use this information to take and fulfil the purchase, confirm the correct licence or entitlement, administer subscriptions and cancellations, resolve billing/support requests, prevent payment abuse, keep accounting/tax evidence and handle legal claims. We do not use agreement to billing terms as consent to unrelated advertising or optional tracking.

Payment and support providers

Stripe processes payment details on its hosted payment surfaces. We receive billing/status information and may see limited payment-method details; we do not ask you to send full card details through our websites or support email. If Managed Payments is used, Link additionally provides transaction/order management and transaction support. Stripe/Link process some information for their own purposes under their privacy notices; they are not described here solely as our processors.

Relevant billing information may be shared with the product system to provide or adjust the purchased entitlement. This includes email delivery of billing confirmations and, where the product provides it, recovery information; it is not a promise that every provider email contains an installer. For honour-system utilities, a stable ordinary download and durable payment evidence support re-access without a new app account. Support email is handled through Migadu. Where used for business bookkeeping and private recovery, Google Drive/Sheets also hold relevant billing records. Where needed, relevant records may be disclosed to accounting or legal service providers, competent authorities or payment/dispute participants for the purposes above. This notice does not authorize unrelated cross-product marketing.

Locations, safeguards and this legal page

We administer records in Switzerland. Stripe’s global services can involve other countries, including the United States and India; its privacy centre and applicable data-protection terms describe relevant entities, recipients and transfer safeguards, including contractual safeguards where applicable. Required safeguards depend on the actual transfer and provider arrangement.

These billing legal pages are hosted at dumptotxt.com using GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security, including visits without a GitHub login. Standard connection/request information is handled under GitHub’s privacy statement. These legal pages add no analytics, form, app login or nonessential tracking script. Other product sites and checkout have their own notices and processing.

Migadu-Mail GmbH is the email-service provider. Its published service description identifies current mail data centres in France, while the provider is based in Switzerland. Email sender/recipient addresses, message contents, attachments and delivery metadata are processed to receive and answer support messages. Its privacy/data-processing policy describes retention for service/legal purposes and deletion within 30 days after its customer service contract ends, subject to exceptions. That is not a promise that our support conversations disappear 30 days after a request. Ordinary email is not represented as end-to-end encrypted.

Google Drive/Sheets, when used for bookkeeping or recovery, are covered by Google’s privacy policy. Google operates globally, including in the United States; its published locations describe its facilities. This is not a promise of Swiss-only or a customer-selected data region.

Our providers may process data outside your country: Migadu identifies France; GitHub and Google describe international processing including the United States; Stripe describes global processing including the United States and other locations for the applicable service. Their linked notices and transfer documentation describe the relevant jurisdictions and safeguards, including standard contractual clauses and applicable data-privacy-framework arrangements. Contact us for information on the safeguards relevant to our handling of your data. We do not promise that every provider uses every listed mechanism or that provider security logs have a universal fixed expiry.

Retention

We keep accounting records and supporting documents for the applicable legal period, generally ten years from the end of the relevant financial year under Swiss accounting rules. Tax rules or a legal hold can require particular records for another period. We keep subscription and agreement-version evidence as needed to administer the contract, meet applicable record duties or resolve claims. Support material not needed for those purposes is removed when the request and relevant claim/retention need have ended. Payment providers apply their own lawful retention periods.

Your rights and contact

Contact the controller above to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability where the applicable law provides that right. We may need proportionate identity verification. Legal retention duties can prevent immediate deletion of particular records. You may complain to the competent data-protection authority, including the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner; applicable EU/EEA rights and supervisory-authority remedies are preserved.

Where EU data-protection law applies, our relevant bases are taking steps for and performing the purchase contract, complying with applicable legal obligations, and legitimate interests in necessary billing administration, security and legal claims, subject to your rights. Separate optional processing requires its own applicable basis. This notice is information, not a request for blanket consent.
