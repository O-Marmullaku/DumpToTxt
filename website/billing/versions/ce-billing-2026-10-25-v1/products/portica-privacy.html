Privacy

Portica is operated by Crazy Entertainment, Hasenweg 9, 5611 Anglikon, Switzerland. This notice explains how information is used when you build, export or publish a site. For privacy questions or requests, email support@remotedroneflying.com.

Updated 7 October 2026

Your account and drafts

We use your email, account identifiers, name and profile image to provide sign-in and your account. If you choose Google or GitHub sign-in, that provider supplies account information through Supabase, which provides our authentication, database and file storage. Resend delivers sign-in emails.

Guest drafts are saved in your browser’s local storage. When you sign in, guest-created drafts are saved to your account and removed from the guest store after a successful transfer. Your browser also stores session information to keep you signed in. Clearing browser storage can remove guest drafts that have not been transferred to an account.

For signed-in accounts, we store the site content and settings you save, uploaded media, retained image originals and restore points. Content can include career details, contact information, projects and testimonials. Earlier text or images may remain in a restore point after you edit the current site.

Optional automatic extraction

When available, automatic extraction sends the source text, PDF or image you select through Supabase to OpenAI, after you choose to share it. You can use manual entry instead. Extracted facts require your approval before they become site content.

The extraction service requests that OpenAI not store the response, and does not save your submitted source in Portica’s database or include it in application logs. This is not a promise of zero retention by OpenAI or other infrastructure providers. Only submit material you have permission to share.

Payments and publishing

When you use paid services, Stripe handles payment details. We share your email and account, site and offer identifiers with Stripe to create and manage checkout. Portica keeps payment and subscription identifiers, amounts, currencies, statuses and refund or dispute information to manage access and billing. Portica does not collect card numbers directly.

Publishing makes your generated site public. Managed sites are served through Cloudflare. GitHub publishing sends the generated files to a public repository in your GitHub account. The GitHub access token from your sign-in session is forwarded for publishing requests; it is not saved in Portica’s database or application logs. Images included in your site are public. Separate original-file downloads and the editable ownership-data JSON are excluded from the public artifact.

Custom-domain setup shares the hostname and account/site identifiers with Cloudflare. External images, YouTube or Vimeo embeds, and checkout links you add may contact those providers when people view or use your site. Their own privacy practices apply.

Service and security records

For recovery, encrypted copies of uploaded media and hosted site files are stored in Cloudflare R2. Automated backups process these files on GitHub-hosted runners. We retain the seven most recent successful media backups; older copies are removed after a new backup succeeds.

We use operational records to run the service, limit abuse and investigate failures. Application logs include timestamps, request and account/site identifiers, operation outcomes and error details. Hosting and infrastructure providers also process request information such as IP addresses, browser information and network location. These providers process information to deliver their services.

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives font requests and associated network information.

Exporting and deleting information

You can export your sites and download Portica data from Account. Portica data deletion removes your Portica records and owned files from active Supabase storage, requests deletion of your Stripe customer, and removes registered Cloudflare custom hostnames. Your shared Lexy sign-in remains available for other apps. Unpublishing takes the managed site offline and removes its stored releases; it does not cancel a hosting subscription.

Deletion does not remove GitHub repositories, downloaded exports or copies held by other people. It does not guarantee immediate removal from provider logs, backups or payment records. Retention by those providers can differ from removal in the app. Contact us about access, correction or deletion requests, including information you cannot manage yourself.
